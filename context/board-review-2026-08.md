# Board review — August 2026

Owner: CEO · Date: 27 August 2026 · Status: approved

## Numbers

- Net revenue retention: 71% (target 80%).
- Logo churn on the Growth plan: 29% over the last 12 months.
- Enterprise renewals due in Q4: 3, including Meridian Brokers (EUR 480k ARR, 24 October 2026).

## Decisions

1. Fix churn before shipping new modules. No new module starts until net revenue retention is back above 80%.
2. Every roadmap item must state the number of accounts it touches. One-account features are refused unless contractually committed.
3. Product decisions are made on evidence from product usage, not on the volume of requests.
