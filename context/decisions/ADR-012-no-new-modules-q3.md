# ADR-012 — No new modules in Q3 2026

Status: accepted · Date: 28 August 2026 · Deciders: CEO, CTO, Head of Product

## Context

Net revenue retention is at 71%. The two developers on T-REX are the same people who would build any new module. The August board review asked for churn to be fixed first.

## Decision

The team does not start any new module — any body of work sized L or XL — before net revenue retention is above 80%. A request for a module is answered with the smallest change that covers the underlying need.

## Consequences

Module requests are not refused silently: they are re-scoped, and the customer is told what ships instead and when the module is reconsidered.
